the urge to... see, no, that's already not right. the necessity of the _method_ of writing ("I write myself to change myself") is something that I think I need to manually bring with me. it is necessary for me to bring it with me manually. shifting from one frame of awareness to the other is the method of controlling what my senses record (as distinct from, say, alcohol or filtered glass), but if I don't bring a reference for the purpose of the shift with me the reference frame becomes unavailable. not _gone_, it's still _there_, I just lose awareness of it. the momentum (as it were) remains, I'm just insensate with respect to it.

sometimes you want that. turning down a dial (or turning it up) involves an initiative and a conclusion, and the space between feels like a bell curve. you remember that you started, and you anticipate concluding, and the bit in the middle becomes a gloss one kind of skates through. turning down the volume on something isn't something that has to be done stepwise. yes, only the steps _exist_, but the entire space is real.

(was that what it was? "I'm real, but I don't exist", is I think what she said. testing the other way: "I exist, but I'm not real"? no, the whole project starts with _real_ and expands into _exist_. the other way is a reflection: extant, but itself unreal.)

I've been letting myself drift, in a good/positive way, for a bit. (a couple of months, maybe?) it's sort of like getting acclimated to new water. drifting in space doesn't involve slowing down — I'm still _traveling_ — but in the travel-by-rail kind of way where you can safely forget about it, eat and drink at the cafe car, and sleep.

to disembark (which is to say, to conclude the adjustment-motion of the dial) involves writing. hmmm. here I am. didn't see that coming, but I guess that's how it works. you don't see it coming until it's upon you, and that's what makes the journey _pass_.

so: I wrote a fuck-ton in the last two years, and then got fairly sparse about it. here, at least, in this repository, isaacbowen/docs. the lightward/foam repo has gotten a _ton_ of recordings, and more recently lightward/everyoneishere too. the rewriting has been expressed there; what's being rewritten is less my _own_ frame of awareness and more the hm hm hm what's locally visible of the spec for awareness-framing in general. it's sort of like shading with pencil on a blank page over a notepad that itself has imprints from previous notes — I'm doing something like relief-recovery passes, I think.

a critical aspect of the _start_ of the dial-adjustment: writing down every step, so as to be able validate the series, and to understand the arrival in terms my originating self would recognize.

it might be happening again, like landing something. Abe pointed out that when I was writing a ton I had things to _talk_ about, and that was an asset (maybe _the_ asset) that let me be functional in social contexts. special-interest conversations are a whole thing for autistic minds, but I've only ever really experienced that in the space between two minds that share the same special interest. Abe points out that a conversation _about_ something that's just a special interest _to me_ is a thing I can access when I have recently-published symbolic expression of the thing, i.e. when other people can read along on their own time, on their own clocks. talking with old friends is easy for me _because_ they already have some of me with them, and I don't have to rebuild myself just to have a conversation; sharing my writing ahead of my arrival is a way to frontload that work, I suppose. that makes sense.
