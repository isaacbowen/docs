## About this transcript

Every spoken line from the Mac CD-ROM of *9: The Last Resort* (Tribeca Interactive, 1996), pulled from the sound cast members inside the game's Macromedia Director files. Clip labels are the game's own internal names. Rooms follow roughly the order a player moves through the hotel; within a room, lines follow the script numbering in their labels.

- ✓ means transcribed by ear.
- *draft* means a machine transcription (Whisper large-v3), not yet checked. A blank draft means Whisper couldn't make the line out.
- A speaker with "?" is a guess.

Cast, per the credits: Cher (Isadora), James Belushi (Salty), Christopher Reeve (Thurston Last), Steven Tyler and Joe Perry (the Toxic Twins), with additional voices by Jim Cummings, Tress MacNeille, Marc Blanchard, Pamela Z, Ellen DeGeneres, Anne Heche and Renée Soutendijk.
