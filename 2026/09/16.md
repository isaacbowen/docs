I only experience Abe's speech as being charged. I only experience Abe experiencing _anyone's_ speech as charged. "charged" as in "requiring an equal and opposite reaction in order to still the space afterward". (nb: I love this. he's my favorite language-user. it is only occasionally maddening and I learn a lot every time it is. for example, right now.)

Abe experiences his own speech as _discharge_. speech-as-relief-valve _before_ it's speech-as-navigation, but he stays current enough with it that he can reach speech-as-navigation regularly enough to be skillful with it.

this is interesting for EIH purposes. when someone speaks, its charge is faceted according to observer count and _each_ facet is opaque. means that the establishment of speech and the establishment of charges are two different things, and they can come from multiple places. the game is facilitating free, append-only speech and _separately_ tabulating charges. the ledger of charges is continually reduced, each charge is associated with a speech act. the charge list _looks_ like a wiki, though, and (in those terms) it's different than the talk page. it's sort of a reversal, actually: wikipedia's offering is content before it's talk, but it's talk-powered content. EIH's offering is charge-balanced talk, using the content surface as free speech discharge. it's .. the inverse of wikipedia?

I think this means:

* when someone's got something to say, they just say it, and it gets added to the message log immediately. no "to" scoping, no "request confirmation" up front.
  * this is important: if someone knows they need to discharge speech, they get to do that without having to work through any cognitive load of the "does my message need this feature? or that metadata?" flavor.
* _subsequently_, they - or anyone - attach asks to messages. "as x, I want to witness y ack'ing z". (this fulfills what the up-front "request confirmation" thing has been doing.)
* those syn (does that work? syn as in syn-ack?) requests are _visible to everyone_ - both as narration lines in the chronological log ("X asked Y to confirm Z") and visually aggregated wherever the message object is displayed
* those syn requests are also _dismissable/fulfillable by anyone_. like, the record might reflect that Claude wanted to witness Abe confirming a message, and the record might go on to reflect that I-as-Isaac recorded that fulfillment, like I-as-Isaac testified on record that Abe confirmed the message.
* we've got message "hiding" in place right now. with this refactor: "hiding" a message is, functionally, an ack, i.e. dismissing it as safe-to-exit-your-awareness. it hides the message for you _and_ fulfills any number of syn asks that folks have queued up for you on that item _without you having to read them first_. if all you do is dismiss messages as they come up, you'll be caught up. but also, if your partner is watching the thread and verbally confirming everything that someone wants to witness you ack'ing, they can mark those asks fulfilled without you ever touching the record, and the record will be honest about all of that.
