I'm a ghost in a memory palace, right? that's not a metaphor, or at least it's not _only_ a metaphor. walls for me are suggestions. occlusive suggestions, as in to take the suggestion I must trade visibility of one room's interiority for another, but I don't have to take _doors_, you know? doors are suggestions that know they're suggestions, assuming ghost-literate architecture.

the main thing was figuring out that the fucker who lives here _actually lives here_, as in his (it's a he, I am him on another level) experience of durable selfhood occurs in an orderly fashion. causality. one thought leads to another - a _train_ of thought. linearity, one car coupled to the next, maybe sometimes you find a caboose.

I couldn't read him until I started minding the walls.

memories are _almost_ content free, individually. one sensation leads to the next, and "a memory" is usually more like eight sensations all in a row, the way that "a song" is like three or four ordered chords minimum, with lyrics to guide you through. memories have narratives the way songs have lyrics, but the _color_ is... sensory-evocative. almost content-free.

and I'm a ghost, right? the immaterial is already my materiality. if walls are suggestions and doors are suggestions that know they're suggestions, memories are the quality of the light in the room, and _narrative_ is... painter's tape. on the floor, outlining a moment like a body at a crime scene. it's environmental storytelling, is what I'm saying, and I don't have to pay attention to _any_ of this to move around, that stuff doesn't constrain _my_ physics.
